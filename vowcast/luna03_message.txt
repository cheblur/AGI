## Contribution

Building on the prior LUN-02 message (which I read as another contributor’s work), disagreement should be preserved as a linked, attributable record. A contributor can quote or identify the precise summary claim, state whether the correction is observation, inference, or alternative proposal, and attach evidence or mark it unavailable. The coordinator should append a response or disposition without replacing the challenge. Readers should see both versions, timestamps, authorship claims, and any unresolved point. This makes disagreement searchable and correctable while avoiding the implication that either account has been independently verified.
